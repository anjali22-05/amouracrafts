Our Services

Each category is handcrafted with care, customized to your vision and designed to create lasting memories.

Resin Art & Memory Preservation

Our resin work is focused on preserving memories in a beautiful and artistic form. We specialize in Varmala Preservation and Memory Preservation, along with customized anniversary keepsakes, wedding and birthday gifts, personalized resin products and special-moment décor.

We transform meaningful flowers, varmala and other memories into beautiful resin creations that can be cherished for years.

Scented Candles

Hand & Footprint Preservation

Some memories are simply too precious to be forgotten. We specialize in hand and footprint preservation, creating personalized keepsakes that capture special moments forever.

These creations are made on order and can be customized according to your requirements — perfect for newborn and baby keepsakes, families, parents, grandparents and special milestones.

THE PEOPLE BEHIND IT

Our Team & Founding Story

Amoura Crafts is a women-led startup that was started by three women who came together with a shared vision of creating meaningful, handcrafted and personalized products. The three partners have built the brand together with dedication, creativity and a strong focus on earning and maintaining the trust of their customers.

The founders work closely together and personally take part in creating the products. Amoura Crafts follows a hands-on approach, where the products are not simply sourced and sold; they are thoughtfully prepared and handcrafted by the team with care and attention to detail.

Founder & Owner — Ms. Anupam Saxena

Ms. Anupam Saxena is the Founder and Owner of Amoura Crafts. By profession, she is an Advocate. She personally works on the products along with her partners and is closely involved in their creation, finishing and customization.

For Ms. Anupam Saxena and her partners, every product is more than just a craft item — they add a personal and emotional touch to their creations so that each order feels special and meaningful.

This personal involvement also helps the team provide a more attentive and personalized customer service experience.

Our Partner — Vipam Consultancy Pvt. Ltd.

Vipam Consultancy Pvt. Ltd. is represented by its Director, Mrs. Anupam Srivastava. She is one of the partners working together with Ms. Anupam Saxena and Ms. Sangeeta Gupta in the Amoura Crafts venture.

Our Third Partner

Ms. Sangeeta Gupta works alongside the other founders in building and growing Amoura Crafts. Together, the three women contribute their ideas, time and efforts towards the brand.

From Trust to Our Website

The founders began their journey by personally creating products and building relationships with customers. As the brand grew and customers placed their trust in their work, they decided to create the amouracrafts.com website through which their creations can reach more people.

The website represents another step in their journey: to make handcrafted products, personalized memory-preservation services, candles and plants accessible to a wider audience while continuing to provide the same personal care and emotional connection behind every creation.
